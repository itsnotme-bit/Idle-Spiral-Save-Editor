04 / Battle upgrades

—

Edit upgrade levels individually. Numbers correspond to their position in the game's battle_upgrade_levels array. Community descriptions are only a guide; later versions may use different names or limits.

The game can enforce upgrade caps or unlock requirements after import. This editor changes saved levels only; it does not unlock associated content, alter purchase costs or guarantee that extreme levels have an effect.
